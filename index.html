Sound familiar?

Running a shop is hard.
Not knowing what to fix is harder.

Every shop owner faces the same nagging questions — but rarely has the tools or time to find real answers.

📍
"Is my location good enough?"
You picked the spot, signed the lease — but you're still not sure if the foot traffic or visibility is holding you back compared to competitors a few blocks away.
🥊
"Why are nearby competitors busier than me?"
The café across the street always has a queue. You can't tell if it's their prices, their Instagram, their reviews, or something else entirely — so you don't know what to copy.
🍽️
"Is my menu or website hurting my sales?"
Your food is great — you know it. But maybe the menu layout, dish naming, pricing structure, or website design is turning customers away before they even try you.
🎯
"What should I even improve first?"
Everyone has advice: post more on Instagram, get more reviews, do a promotion. But with limited time and money, you need to know which action will actually move the needle.
